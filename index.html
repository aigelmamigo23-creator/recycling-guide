Sort it right. Help Mother Earth.

Tap a bin to see what goes in it. Rinse, dry and separate your waste before it leaves your hands.

Biodegradable

Food scraps and plants

Put in

  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Leftover food
  • Eggshells, coffee grounds, tea bags
  • Leaves, grass and yard trimmings
  • Clean paper napkins

Keep out

  • Anything plastic-coated
  • Meat and fish bones, unless your barangay accepts them

Tip: compost it at home or give it to the barangay collection.

Recyclable

Clean and dry only

Put in

  • Paper, cardboard and newspaper
  • PET bottles and HDPE containers
  • Aluminum and tin cans
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Clean plastic bags and sachets, if accepted locally

Keep out

  • Greasy pizza boxes
  • Wet or food-soiled paper

Tip: rinse, dry and flatten, then bring to your MRF or junk shop.

Residual

Non-recyclable trash

Put in

  • Sanitary napkins and diapers
  • Candy and snack wrappers
  • Used tissue and soiled paper
  • Styrofoam and broken ceramics
  • Cigarette butts

Keep out

  • Clean items that can still be recycled

Tip: bag it securely for regular garbage collection.

Special and hazardous

Drop-off items

Put in

  • Batteries and power banks
  • Old phones, chargers and other e-waste
  • Paint, thinner and pesticides
  • Fluorescent bulbs and CFLs
  • Used cooking oil and medical waste

Keep out

  • Regular trash bins. Never mix these with other waste.

Tip: bring them to a designated collection point.

Collection rules differ by barangay, so check with your local office for schedules and drop-off points.
